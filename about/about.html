Our Journey

Legacy of Trust and Excellence

in Dubai and Oman

Infinowa started with a basic concept: to close the distance between individuals and professional technical solutions. From modest origins as a service shop in Dubai, then we grew and developed into the most reliable brands for gadget repairs in Oman, Qatar and Doha. Along the road, we have reached important benchmarks such as servicing thousands of devices, forming alliances with big names, and building a devoted clientele. Originally driven only by a love of mending devices, this enthusiasm has evolved into a dedication to keep our society linked and functional.

Infinowa's journey began in Dubai and Oman, where we started building our expertise in tech repair services. Over the years, we established a reputation for creativity, reliability, and a strong customer-first approach.
